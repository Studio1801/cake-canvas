Terms of service.

These terms cover cakes and desserts ordered from CakeCanvas.co ("we", "us"). By placing a request through this site you agree to them.

Requests and confirmation

Submitting the designer form is a request, not a purchase. Your order is confirmed only when we accept it in writing and receive your deposit. Prices shown in the designer are estimates until confirmed.

Deposits and payment

A non-refundable 50% deposit secures your pickup date. The balance is due at pickup or before delivery.

Changes and cancellations

  • Design changes are welcome up to five days before pickup.
  • Cancellations seven or more days before pickup may move the deposit to a future date once.
  • Cancellations within seven days forfeit the deposit.

Design interpretation

Each cake is made by hand. Colours, flowers and finishes may vary slightly from photos, sketches and screen colours. Fresh flowers are decorative and not for eating.

Allergens

Our kitchen handles wheat, eggs, dairy, soy and nuts. We take care with special requests but cannot guarantee an allergen free product.

Pickup, delivery and storage

Once a cake is collected or delivered, transport and storage are your responsibility. We will share care instructions with every order.

Privacy policy.

We collect only what we need to make and hand over your cake.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone number or email address.

How we use it

To reply to your request, confirm your order, send pickup reminders and answer questions. We do not sell or rent your information.

Payments

Deposits are handled by our payment provider. We never see or store your full card number.

How long we keep it

Order details are kept for up to two years for records and repeat orders. Ask us at any time and we will delete them.
